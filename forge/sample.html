SAMPLE REVISION — LAYOUT CHANGES EXPLAINED Competitive analysis of theringlord.com and alchemygothic.com against the current forge.html surfaced 5 structural gaps.
  1. FULL-WIDTH BRAND HERO — Original 2-column text header replaced with cinematic wide hero that leads with the brand name large and includes CTAs at first scroll. Viking/rave jewelry sites establish visual identity before prose.
  2. ORDER FLOW MADE EXPLICIT — New 3-step commission flow (describe → size → receive) placed before price cards. Competitive sites surface the HOW-TO-BUY path prominently because custom ordering intimidates customers.
  3. SKU CARDS ELEVATED + EARLIER — Price cards now appear directly after the order flow, before long prose sections. Product + price is conversion-critical content; burying it after text paragraphs loses customers mid-scroll.
  4. ASYMMETRIC GALLERY MOSAIC — Six identical placeholder boxes replaced with an intentional 5-card mosaic (1 featured wide + 4 standard). Each placeholder slot carries contextual weave labels so the grid reads as curated, not empty.
  5. COMMISSION AS STANDALONE BRAND STATEMENT — Custom commissions pulled out of the materials section into its own hero panel with a direct email CTA. For a made-to-order craft brand, commissions IS the product.
