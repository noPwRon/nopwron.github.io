LED REACTIVE LEATHER HIP BAG SYSTEM IN BUILD

A hand-stitched leather hip bag with removable LED electronics module. Laser-cut decorative panels with translucent acrylic inserts diffuse light when the module is installed — and look exceptional without it. Controlled via a custom-branded mobile app over Bluetooth LE. Built for festivals, raves, circus, and live performance culture. Modular by design — the electronics slide out for washing, water exposure, or days you want the leather alone.
